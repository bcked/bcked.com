# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

## Controller

The provider named in the [Legal Notice](/legal-notice).
Email: [contact@bcked.com](mailto:contact@bcked.com)

## Cookies, tracking and browser storage

bcked.com sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking and loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
To restore the scroll position when you go back and forward between pages, the website keeps the
scroll positions in your browser's session storage. They contain no personal data, never leave your
browser and are deleted when you close the tab. This storage is technically necessary for the
website (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

## Hosting

The website (bcked.com) and the API it loads its data from (api.bcked.com) are hosted on GitHub Pages
by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your
browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address, the requested page, the
time and your browser's user agent. GitHub uses this data to deliver the pages and to keep the service
secure, and stores it in server logs. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in a secure and
reliable website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under
the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision under Art. 45 GDPR). Details:
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Contact by email

If you write an email, your address and message are used only to answer you (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f)
GDPR) and are deleted when they are no longer needed, unless legal retention periods apply. Emails to
contact@bcked.com are stored with Microsoft (Outlook.com).

## GitHub issues and pull requests

Corrections and token requests are handled on GitHub. What you post there is public and is processed
by GitHub under its own terms and privacy statement.

## Links to other websites

The website links to social media profiles (X/Twitter, Reddit, Telegram, GitHub) and to the sources of
the data. These are plain links. No data is sent to these services until you click a link. Their own
privacy policies then apply.

## Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction
of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate
interests (Art. 21). Write to [contact@bcked.com](mailto:contact@bcked.com).

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for
the controller: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit
Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.
